Executive Summary

Open Mind Center (OMC) is a former retreat and meditation center in Pai, Thailand, being revived as a Behavioral Intelligence-enhanced co-living and retreat community. We are raising $180,000 in bridge financing, structured as 100% interest-bearing loans (5% per annum, 1-year term), to complete the final payment on the property and secure full ownership outright. The land and existing buildings - including two currently income-generating villas - stand behind the loan as security.

At maturity, lenders individually choose either full repayment with interest, or conversion into an OMC equity-equivalent with revenue share at the then-current valuation, positioned ahead of a planned mid-2027 crowdfunding round targeting approximately $600,000 for renovation and expansion.

This bridge round funds land acquisition only; it does not include renovation costs, which will be raised separately once ownership is secured. Beyond the immediate opportunity, OMC is envisioned as an extension of the Hummingbird Behavioral Intelligence ecosystem - a founding community of up to 118 members across four participation pathways, built around Behavioral Intelligence coaching and a gradual trust-building journey from first contact to long-term co-living membership.

Context

We are currently raising bridge financing to secure full ownership of the Open Mind Center property and buildings in Pai, Thailand. The land is currently held by the seller, with 25% of the purchase price already paid. The remaining 75% must be paid to complete the purchase and secure the property outright. Of that remaining balance, a portion is already committed through our Thai land partner, leaving a defined funding gap. For that gap, we are inviting private investors to provide interest-bearing loans, with the option — at the lender's discretion — to convert the loan into an equity-equivalent at maturity.

About the Property - Open Mind Center

Open Mind Center (OMC) is a former retreat and meditation center that operated for many years before being left abandoned during the COVID era. We have taken over the property and signed an agreement for full ownership by the end of 2026 — though the seller's current legal situation requires us to move faster than originally planned and complete the final payment now rather than later.

The property includes four intact bungalows, each with river views and sweeping vistas of the surrounding mountain range and the Pai Canyon, one of the region's most iconic natural landmarks. Set slightly outside the town center, the location is exceptionally quiet — an ideal setting for retreats and immersive nature experiences. A meditation hall and a spacious main building (large enough to house a restaurant, communal spaces, and a kitchen) anchor the site, complemented by expansive terraces and panoramic views throughout the grounds.

Investment Proposal - Bridge Funding Loan

1. Legal Structure

  • OMC is a separate Thai legal entity, owned by the seller.
  • OMC owns the land (including the retreat center buildings and the two existing villas) directly.
  • Once the sale is completed, the property and buildings will be transferred and owned 99% by HUMMINGBIRD HOTELS PAIRADISE CO., LTD, (Thai entity) and 1% by our second Thai partner Pasit.

2. Current and intended Financial Model

  • OMC is pre-revenue. There is no financial projection or revenue model finalized yet.
  • Two villas on the property are currently rented out via Airbnb — a small income the property generates today.
  • Long-term intention: develop OMC into a combination of co-living village and retreat center / hotel / resort for retreats, workshops, and hospitality, following a similar model as Pairadise Resort.
  • Investor security: the land with the existing buildings and the two villas themselves stand behind the bridge loan. After the land is bought, another round of fundraising will begin to cover a major renovation to generate meaningful and sustainable revenue.

4. Current Valuation

  • Basis for current valuation: the purchase price of the land.
  • Upgrades, investments, and already completed renovations (>$15,000) not taken into account.
  • At today's actual land purchasing cost, 1% equity = $4,800, which is below its market value.
  • The market value would include the 2 years of running the villas on Airbnb with possible returning customers and all the upgrades and repairs made during this period, with a conservative estimate of $5,500 per 1%.
  • However, we are setting the value at only $5,000 per 1% to express goodwill and appreciation of the time-sensitive critical support.
  • If OMC raises further funds next year at a higher valuation, 1% equity is expected to reprice upward - roughly $6,000 per 1% based on current projections (~20% ROI)

Appendix 1 - Future Funding Strategy & Community Development (2027)

A crowdfunding round where participants pre-pay for their stay is planned for approximately one year from now (target: mid-2027), with a fundraising goal of approximately $600,000.

The funds will be used to:

  • Repay $189,000 in loans, including $9,000 in accrued interest.
  • Allocate $11,000 to compensate the humans who put work into the fund-raising campaign.
  • Allocate approximately $100,000 for operational costs, including on-site renovation management, day-to-day property management, and operational support during the redevelopment phase.
  • Fund building additional bungalows, rooms, renovations and infrastructure improvements, budgeted as the remaining approximately $300,000 of the $600,000 after operation cost (viable at the minimum of $200,000 if the full fundraising goal is not achieved).
  • Establish a valuation and conversion reference point for existing and future lenders who may wish to convert their loans into equity-equivalent with revenue share in future financing rounds.

As a contingency plan, if the crowdfunding campaign does not reach its minimum objectives, the property remains a valuable underlying asset and can be sold to fully repay the outstanding loan.

Infrastructure and Building Development

The $300,000 (viable at a minimum of $200,000) will allow us to renovate and upgrade the current existing structures and build additional bungalows and accommodations for up to 60+ people with private accommodations.

Appendix 2 - Community Building - The "Path of Human Flourishing" Journey

Our intention is to develop an entrepreneurial community of committed cocreators for long-term co-living. Beyond just creating a model for living in harmony with each other, the land and the local community, we strive to become a generative hub pioneering various projects and creating an ecosystem of ventures to support human flourishing and improve the quality of life in areas like movement, eating, connection, work, sleep and others.

We are inviting value-aligned humans through a crowdfunding campaign to invest in their future stay at the center (2028 onward) and use the generated funds to accomplish the renovations and ensure the operational readiness of the place.

We are inviting a community focused on human flourishing that bridges science, spirituality, art, and technology. Based on the inner technology of Behavioral Intelligence (BI), we are developing a transformational journey that is both curating the right constellation of people and, at the same time, continuously building capacity for the people joining on this path.

If you like to learn more about Behavioral Intelligence, please look at the attached vision deck that expands on the concept in depth.

Interpersonal challenges are not only the main reason for failed communities and projects but also the biggest cost factor when it comes to working together. We are focusing the entire co-creation journey on expanding our awareness and capacity to deal with our often times outdated beliefs and strategies proactively. For us, living and working together is not only measured by the outcome of projects and businesses but mostly and foremost, by the personal growth and transformation that we are experiencing.

Founding Community (118 Humans)

Target:
58 members (plus 60 retreat participants)

Participants who resonate deeply with the vision may choose to become members of the Founding Community through the crowdfunding campaign. They pay a fixed amount in 2027 to finance the upgrade and renovations which guarantees them a corresponding number of nights to stay at the community for two years once completed, approximately 2028 onwards.

Instead of offering a single membership model, we provide four participation pathways, allowing people to choose the level of involvement that best fits their lifestyle while helping build the community together.

Participation Pathways

Founder's Path (8 people)

Time Commitment: 250 nights per year

  • Total accommodation contribution (2 years): $10,000
  • Effective rate: approximately $20 per night

Designed for people who wish to make OMC their primary home and actively contribute to building the community.

Examples: Founders such as Ting and Jonathan

Co-Creator's Path (20 people)

Time Commitment: 150 nights per year

  • Total accommodation contribution (2 years): $9,000
  • Effective rate: approximately $30 per night

For members who want to spend significant time living, working, and contributing to the ecosystem while maintaining another home elsewhere.

Explorer's Path (30 people)

Time Commitment: 75 nights per year

  • Total accommodation contribution (2 years): $6,000
  • Effective rate: approximately $40 per night

Ideal for digital nomads, entrepreneurs, creators, and remote workers who want to immerse themselves in the community through several extended stays each year.

Retreat Path (60 people)

Time Commitment: 15 nights per year

  • Total accommodation contribution (2 years): $3,000
  • Effective rate: approximately $100 per night
  • not including retreat participation fees

Designed for people primarily seeking restoration, learning, coaching, and transformation while becoming part of the broader OMC network.

Many participants may begin through retreats before later choosing one of the longer-term community pathways.

Appendix 3 - About Us - Who We Are
